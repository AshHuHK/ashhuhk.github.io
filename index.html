About me

I am an Assistant Professor, PhD Supervisor, and Research Scientist at the School of Information Science and Technology, Great Bay University.

My research develops artificial intelligence methods for computational biology, focusing on the structure and function modeling of RNA, proteins, and other biomolecules. My previous work spans representation learning, structure prediction, homolog retrieval, and molecular design, with publications in Nature Methods, Nature Biotechnology, and Nature Computational Science.

My future research will address related challenging questions in biomolecular dynamics, conformational heterogeneity, cellular-context-aware modeling, RNA localization and function, molecular design, transcriptome-scale modeling, and virtual cell systems.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Yu Li and Prof. Irwin King. I received my B.S. in Mathematics from Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

Research interests

关于我

我现任大湾区大学信息科学技术学院助理教授、博士生导师、研究员。

我的研究主要发展面向计算生物学的人工智能方法，重点关注 RNA、蛋白质等生物大分子的结构与功能建模。此前的工作围绕大分子表征学习、结构预测、同源检索和分子设计展开，相关成果发表于 Nature Methods、Nature Biotechnology 和 Nature Computational Science 等期刊。

未来，我的课题组将继续关注计算生物学中的关键难题，包括生物大分子的动态过程、构象异质性、细胞环境感知建模、RNA 在细胞内的定位与功能、分子设计、转录组尺度建模，以及面向虚拟细胞体系的分子层机制建模。

博士毕业于香港中文大学计算机科学专业，导师为 Yu Li 教授 和 Irwin King 教授；本科毕业于上海交通大学数学专业。
