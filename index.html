About me

I am an Assistant Professor (Principle Investigator) at the School of Computing and Information Technology, Great Bay University since Summer, 2026. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Yu Li and Prof. Irwin King. I received my B.S. in Mathematics from Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

My research develops artificial intelligence methods for computational biology, focusing on the structure and function modeling of RNA, proteins, and other biomolecules. My previous work spans representation learning, biological language models, structure prediction, homolog retrieval, and molecular design, with highlighted publications in Nature Methods, Nature Biotechnology, Nature Computational Science and other top AI conference

My future research will address related challenging questions in biomolecular dynamics, conformational heterogeneity, cellular-context-aware modeling, RNA localization and function, molecular design, transcriptome-scale modeling, and virtual cell systems.

Research interests

  • Structure Modelling
  • RNA–protein interaction
  • Molecular design and computational biology
  • Biological foundation models
  • Virtual cells

My research path

关于我

我于2026年夏天加入大湾区大学信息科学技术学院担任助理教授(独立PI)。 在此之前，我博士毕业于香港中文大学计算机科学专业，导师为 Yu Li 教授 和 Irwin King 教授；本科毕业于上海交通大学数学专业。

我的研究主要发展面向计算生物学的人工智能方法，重点关注 RNA、蛋白质等生物大分子的结构与功能建模。此前的工作围绕大分子表征学习、生物大模型、结构预测、同源检索和分子设计展开，相关亮眼成果发表于 Nature Methods、Nature Biotechnology 、 Nature Computational Science 等子刊和AI顶会。

在此之上，我的课题组未来将继续关注计算生物学中的关键难题，包括生物大分子建模的动态过程、构象异质性、在细胞环境中的建模、定位与功能、分子设计、转录组尺度建模，以及面向虚拟细胞体系的分子层机制建模。

研究方向

  • 结构预测
  • RNA–蛋白互作
  • 药物设计
  • 生物大模型
  • 虚拟细胞
