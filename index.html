About me

I am an Assistant Professor, PhD Supervisor, and Research Scientist at the School of Information Science and Technology, Great Bay University.

My research develops artificial intelligence methods for computational RNA biology, with a focus on RNA structure modeling, RNA–protein interaction modeling, and molecular design.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Yu Li and Prof. Irwin King. I received my B.S. in Mathematics from Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

Research interests

  • AI for RNA structure and cellular-context modeling
  • RNA–protein interaction and interface prediction
  • Molecular design and computational biology

关于我

我现任大湾区大学信息科学技术学院助理教授、博士生导师、研究员。

我的研究聚焦于面向 RNA 计算生物学的人工智能方法，主要包括 RNA 结构建模、RNA–蛋白互作建模与分子设计。

博士毕业于香港中文大学计算机科学专业，导师为 Yu Li 教授 和 Irwin King 教授；本科毕业于上海交通大学数学专业。

研究方向

  • 面向 RNA 结构与细胞环境建模的人工智能方法
  • RNA–蛋白互作与界面预测
  • 分子设计与计算生物学
