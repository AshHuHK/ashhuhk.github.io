About me

关于我

Research profile个人简介

I am an Assistant Professor, PhD Supervisor, and Research Scientist at Great Bay University. My research centers on AI for computational RNA biology and biomolecular modeling, with a focus on building generalizable, interpretable, and designable models for RNA structure and function.

My previous work includes RNA-FM, RhoFold/RhoFold+, RhoDesign, and DHR, spanning large-scale RNA representation learning, RNA 3D structure prediction, functional RNA sequence design, and efficient homolog retrieval.

我现任大湾区大学助理教授、博士生导师、研究员。研究方向聚焦 AI for RNA 计算生物学与大分子结构建模，目标是建立具有泛化性、可解释性和可设计性的 RNA 结构与功能建模方法。

代表性工作包括 RNA-FM、RhoFold/RhoFold+、RhoDesign 和 DHR，覆盖大规模 RNA 表征学习、RNA 三维结构预测、功能 RNA 序列设计以及高效同源检索。

Research interests

研究方向

AI for RNA structure, interaction, and design面向 RNA 结构、互作与设计的人工智能方法

01

RNA Foundation Models

Self-supervised RNA representation learning for structure, function, and cross-family generalization.

基于自监督学习的 RNA 通用表征，用于结构、功能预测与跨家族泛化。

02

RNA 3D Structure

End-to-end RNA 3D structure prediction with learned sequence representations and geometric constraints.

结合序列表征与几何约束，发展端到端 RNA 三维结构预测方法。

03

RNA-Protein Interaction

Modeling nucleotide-residue contacts and interpretable recognition mechanisms in cellular contexts.

解析 RNA—蛋白互作中的核苷酸—残基接触界面和可解释识别机制。

04

Functional RNA Design

Structure-guided generative design of functional RNA molecules and aptamers.

面向功能核酸与适配体的结构引导式生成设计。

05

Dense Homolog Retrieval

Scalable homolog search and evolutionary information extraction using language-model representations.

利用大模型表征进行高效同源检索与进化信息提取。

06

Virtual Cell Mechanisms

RNA structural-state modeling in cellular environments and viral RNA-host RBP mechanisms.

面向细胞环境的 RNA 多状态结构建模与病毒 RNA—宿主 RBP 机制解析。
