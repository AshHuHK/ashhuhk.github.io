About Me

关于我

Machine learning for biomolecular structure, interfaces, and molecular discovery.

机器学习用于生物大分子结构、互作与分子发现。

I am an Assistant Professor, PhD Supervisor, and Research Scientist at the School of Information Science and Technology, Great Bay University. My research develops machine learning models for biomolecular structure, interaction, and design problems, with a focus on RNA structural states, RNA–protein interfaces, and biologically grounded molecular discovery.

我现任大湾区大学信息科学技术学院助理教授、博士生导师、研究员。我的研究主要发展用于生物大分子结构、互作与设计问题的机器学习模型，重点关注 RNA 结构状态、RNA—蛋白互作界面，以及具有生物学背景的分子发现问题。
