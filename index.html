About Me

关于我

Assistant Professor at Great Bay University

大湾区大学助理教授

I am an Assistant Professor, PhD Supervisor, and Research Scientist at the School of Information Science and Technology, Great Bay University. My research develops AI and machine learning methods for computational biology, with a focus on RNA structure, RNA–protein interaction, and biomolecular design.

我现任大湾区大学信息科学技术学院助理教授、博士生导师、研究员。我的研究主要发展面向计算生物学的人工智能与机器学习方法，关注 RNA 结构、RNA—蛋白互作与生物大分子设计。
